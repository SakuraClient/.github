<h1 align="center">Sakura Client</h1>

<p align="center">Minecraft, in bloom.</p>

Sakura Client is a Minecraft client made with care: a HUD that reads anywhere, your music over the game, photo mode, friends’ worlds and capes. Free during the beta, on Windows 10 and 11.

**Download it only from [sakuraclient.com](https://sakuraclient.com).** Sakura is never posted on GitHub or file-sharing sites as a download, and there is no Sakura “repack”, loader or cracked build. ### For server owners

[Sakura Integration](https://github.com/SakuraClient/sakuraintegration) lets your server work with Sakura players: turn Sakura’s mods off where they don’t fit, see who plays on Sakura, and verify your address. Paper, Spigot and Velocity.
